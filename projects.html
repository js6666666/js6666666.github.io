01 / Clinical language

Published work

Learning from clinical narratives

Clinical notes contain information that can be difficult to represent in structured records. My collaborative work explores long-document classification and learning across structured and unstructured EHR data.

IRIS uses retrieval to classify long document sequences. Related multimodal work studies how models can learn from notes during training while using structured records at deployment.

02 / Child development

Published work

Late talking & diagnostic timing

How do speech-language concerns documented in clinical notes relate to formal diagnostic coding? This work uses clinical NLP to study pediatric notes and the timing of speech-language delay diagnoses.

The broader research direction considers what longitudinal health records can tell us about children’s language development.
